Summary Background 16
Summary
Molten regolith containment
• YSZ dissolution and Y-depletion are
primary degradation mechanisms
• Y-depletion causes YSZ to become
monoclinic on cooling, leading to
cracking
• YSZ can be used to contain molten regolith
for limited durations and for a single
thermal cycle
• YSZ near interface with molten regolith
undergoes grain coarsening
• May slow rate of regolith penetration
along grain boundaries
Hollow anode for MRE
• Proof-of-concept experiment successfully
demonstrated O2 (anode) + Fe (cathode)
production
• Hollow anode allows O2 to be easily
collected after being produced through MRE
• Avoids secondary reactions from
bubbling O2 through molten regolith
• YSZ can be used as a solid electrolyte in
the hollow anode design for limited
durations and single thermal cycle
• Future work will focus on extending
MRE durations
