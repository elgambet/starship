## Document files

The seven PDFs in `sources/` are unmodified downloaded primary-source documents. The PNGs above are rendered pages at readable resolution. Their source URLs and page indices are recorded in the table so another agent can re-render or inspect the full context. Full text extracts are available in [`sources-md/`](sources-md/), with a short [PDF index](PDF_INDEX.md) for the HLS-relevant pages.

## Research links without local image copies

- [SpaceX 2025 HLS update](https://www.spacex.com/updates): more than 600 m³ total pressurized habitable volume, two approximately 13 m³ airlocks, full-scale cabin and airlock/elevator testing.
- [SpaceX Moon page](https://www.spacex.com/humanspaceflight/moon): Starship HLS mission and official cabin art.
- [SpaceX Starship vehicle page](https://www.spacex.com/vehicles/starship): 52 m upper stage and nominal 9 m diameter; distinguish the upper stage from the 124 m full launch stack.
- [NASA HLS engineer interview, November 2026](https://www.nasa.gov/podcasts/houston-we-have-a-podcast/artemis-iii-paving-the-way-to-lunar-landings/): approximate 170 ft height, 30 ft width and 22,000 ft³ interior volume; explicit cabin / two airlocks / garage relationship.
- [NASA full-scale airlock test story](https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/esdmd/artemis-campaign-development-division/human-landing-system-program/nasa-astronauts-practice-next-giant-leap-for-artemis/): path and human-factor tests, with no published dimensions.
- [NASA July 2026 Artemis III test description](https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/esdmd/artemis-campaign-development-division/human-landing-system-program/how-nasas-artemis-iii-lander-test-will-pave-way-for-moon-landings/): 52 m test article and changed mission plan.
- [NASA October 2026 mission update](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/artemis/nasa-partners-accelerate-lander-development-for-2028-moon-missions/): no specific lander assigned to first crewed lunar landing.
